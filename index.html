PORTAL DIALER

Travel to any system, in any galaxy

Every star system in No Man's Sky has a 12-glyph portal address. Tap the glyphs on the ring to build one, or roll a random destination.

The same 12 glyphs lead somewhere different in each of the 256 galaxies. And a portal only sends you somewhere inside the galaxy you're already in.
1
Travel in-game. While you're in that galaxy, enter the same 12 glyphs at any portal and you'll be sent there.
2
See where it is. Hit Show on map and the Galactic Map opens and flies straight to that system in 3D.
3
Share it. Share link gives friends a link that opens this dialer with the same address and galaxy already set.
